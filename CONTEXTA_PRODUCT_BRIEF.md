# Contexta Product Brief v0.1

**Target audience:** students preparing for a short-term overseas exchange who already know basic vocabulary but lack situational speaking confidence.

**First market hypothesis:** Korean-language preparation for exchange students; validate through interviews and usability testing.

**Core job to be done:** practice common interactions and learn whether a communication objective was achieved before departure.

**MVP scope:** eight situational tasks, AI roleplay, completion feedback. These are supported through adaptation of the existing application; profile, personalization, analytics and scheduling are future features.

**Success measures to validate:** task completion rate, task completion without hints, perceived usefulness of feedback, repeat-attempt improvement, median response latency, and cost per completed task. No observed metrics are claimed yet.

**Product risks:** model-judged completion may be inconsistent; voice recognition and cultural appropriateness need human review; cloud costs and privacy disclosure must be addressed before user trials.
